## Important wording notes

- Feeding America's full claim is that $1 **helps provide at least 10 meals** secured on behalf of partner food banks.
- Water for Life Charity says its $50 filter can last **3-5+ years** with proper maintenance. The previous "lifetime water" wording was removed because it overstated the source.
- World Vision says catalog amounts are suggested donation amounts for a gift category and funds may be used where most needed within that category. The site reflects that nuance for bed nets and fruit trees.
- Catalog prices can change. Recheck all primary-source links before changing an amount and review the full catalog at least twice per year.
